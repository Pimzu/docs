### In-Game Payment System

**Pay** is a sleek, modern payment resource designed to bring a polished checkout experience directly into your FiveM server.

Instead of relying on clunky external payment flows, Pay gives players a clean and seamless interface whenever a payment is requested by another resource.

---

## ✨ Features

- 💳 Modern, polished checkout interface
- 🎮 Seamless in-game payment experience
- 💰 Supports dynamic payment amounts
- 📝 Custom payment descriptions
- 🔗 Designed to integrate with other resources
- ⚡ Fast and lightweight
- 🔒 Secure payment verification
- 🖥️ Responsive, modern NUI
- 🛠️ Easy developer integration
- 📦 Centralized payment system for your server

---

## 🔌 Built for Integration

Pay is designed to work alongside your existing FiveM resources.

Other scripts can request payments through Pay while providing their own amount and payment description, allowing you to use one consistent checkout experience throughout your entire server.

---

## 💡 Use Pay Anywhere

Pay can be incorporated into a wide variety of systems, including:

- Vehicle-related purchases
- Business payments
- Custom services
- Premium server features
- Storefront systems
- Player purchases
- Custom resources
- And much more

---

## 🎨 One Checkout. Every Resource.

Instead of building a separate payment interface for every resource, **Pay gives your server one centralized, polished payment experience that can be used wherever you need it.**

Keep your payment experience consistent, modern, and easy for your players.
