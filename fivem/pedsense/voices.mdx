title: "🔊 Add Voices"
---

Manage custom NPC voice packs for PedSense. Add your own voice actors, organize dialogue, and test playback directly from the editor.

## 📁 Step 1: Navigate To Voices Folder

Go to folder and decide gender:

<Tree>
  <Tree.Folder name="html">
    <Tree.Folder name="voices">
      <Tree.Folder name="male" />
      <Tree.Folder name="female" />
    </Tree.Folder>
  </Tree.Folder>
</Tree>

## ➕ Step 2: Create A New Folder

Name the folder:

<Tree>
  <Tree.Folder name="html">
    <Tree.Folder name="voices">
      <Tree.Folder name="male">
        <Tree.Folder name="example" />
      </Tree.Folder>
      <Tree.Folder name="female">
        <Tree.Folder name="example" />
      </Tree.Folder>
    </Tree.Folder>
  </Tree.Folder>
</Tree>

## 🫳 Step 3: Put All Audio files in this folder

<Tree>
  <Tree.Folder name="html">
    <Tree.Folder name="voices">
      <Tree.Folder name="male">
        <Tree.Folder name="example">
          <Tree.File name="hello.mp3" />
          <Tree.File name="goodbye.mp3" />
        </Tree.Folder>
      </Tree.Folder>
      <Tree.Folder name="female">
        <Tree.File name="hello.mp3" />
        <Tree.File name="goodbye.mp3" />
      </Tree.Folder>
    </Tree.Folder>
  </Tree.Folder>
</Tree>

<Note>
  Try to keep the names short you will need them later
</Note>

## 🔄 Step 4: Restart PedSense
