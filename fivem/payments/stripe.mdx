How To Set Up Stripe To Take Payments

1. Create an Account For [Stripe](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register)
2. Click "Set Up In The Dashboard"
3. Click  "Get You Live Account Now"
4. Complete The Onboarding Steps
5. You can find your keys on the right in the dashboard
