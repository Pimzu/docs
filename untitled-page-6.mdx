### Get Face

Gets a saved face from the Pedsense face library.

**By Name:**

```text
local face = exports["pedsense"]:getface({
	Gender = "female",
	Tag = "Workers",
	Name = "Employee"

})
```

**Returns:**

- `face` — Saved face data.<br />
- `name` — Saved face name.<br />
- `tag` — Face tag.<br />
- `key` — Face key.<br />

---

### Wear Face

Sets a Pedsense face on a ped.

```text
local ped, gender = exports["pedsense"]:wearface({
	Ped = ped,
	Gender = "female",
	Face = "Employee",
	Tag = "Workers"
})
```
